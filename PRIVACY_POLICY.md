# Privacy Policy for The Shack PH Bot Development Team bots

**Effective date:** October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **The Shack PH Bot Development Team** ("The Team," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information processed by all current and future bots operated under The Shack PH Bot Development Team (collectively, the "Bots").

By using a Bot in a Discord server, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy.

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on the Bot and the features enabled in a server, we may process:

- Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, and bot IDs;
- Discord display names associated with recorded events;
- Message content or message embeds needed to detect supported drop or wish events;
- Event types, reward or count amounts, and UTC timestamps;
- Bot configuration and command-related information needed to respond to requests; and
- Technical information provided by Discord when a command or interaction is received.

We do not ask for passwords, payment card numbers, authentication codes, or Discord account tokens.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Operate, maintain, and secure the Bots;
- Respond to commands and interactions;
- Track supported drop and wish events;
- Provide statistics, counters, and leaderboards requested by server members;
- Prevent duplicate event records and troubleshoot errors;
- Enforce the Terms of Service and protect users and communities; and
- Improve reliability and functionality.

We do not sell personal information or use Bot data for targeted advertising.

## 3. What Is Stored

For Bots that provide tracking, statistics, economy, moderation, or similar features, a Bot may store a server ID, source bot ID, member ID, display name, event type, amount, message ID, configuration values, and UTC timestamp in a database. The exact information stored depends on the Bot and the features enabled in a server.

The Bot processes relevant message content or embeds to identify supported events. The full message content is not stored as part of the event record unless a feature explicitly says otherwise.

## 4. Discord and Service Providers

The Bots operate through Discord's platform and APIs. Discord independently processes information under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We may rely on hosting, infrastructure, backup, or monitoring providers to operate the Bots. Those providers may process information only as reasonably necessary to provide their services.

We do not control Discord's or third-party providers' privacy practices. Review their policies separately.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain event and configuration records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's features, maintain statistics, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain backups.

A server owner or authorized administrator may request deletion of that server's Bot data. Individual users may request deletion of records associated with their Discord account where technically and legally feasible. Requests may require verification and may not remove information that must be retained for security, legal, dispute-resolution, or backup purposes.

To make a request, contact **The Shack PH Bot Development Team through the official The Shack Discord server** and identify the relevant server, Bot, and Discord account without sending passwords or tokens.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect Bot data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Bots are not directed to children in violation of applicable law or Discord's policies. Users must meet Discord's minimum age requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a Bot, contact the Team through the official The Shack Discord server.

## 8. International Processing

Information may be processed or stored in locations where The Team or its service providers operate. Those locations may have data-protection rules different from the rules where you live.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Bot features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this location with a revised effective date. Continued use of a Bot after an update means you acknowledge the updated Policy.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, data-access requests, deletion requests, or concerns about the Bots, contact **The Shack PH Bot Development Team through the official The Shack Discord server**.

This Policy describes the current intended practices of the Bots and is not legal advice. Review it for compliance with the laws applicable to your organization and users before publishing it.
